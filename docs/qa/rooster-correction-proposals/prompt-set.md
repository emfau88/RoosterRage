# Rooster correction proposal prompt set

All concept images in this folder were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. They are preproduction references and are not runtime assets.

## Bummbert turnaround

```text
Create one polished horizontal turnaround sheet of exactly three full-body neutral standing views of the same Bummbert rooster warrior: SOUTH/front, WEST/left-facing side profile, NORTH/back. Preserve his orange, cream, red, bronze and dark-steel identity. Restore the compact original silhouette with a large broad torso, thick neck, large head, and short sturdy legs 20–25% shorter than the current high-detail version. In SOUTH, feet point forward or slightly outward. In WEST, both feet and all long toes point left while one foot may stand ahead of the other. In NORTH, heels are visible and toes point away. Center head, torso, pelvis, tail and stance; attach the tail low at the rump. Integrate rear shoulder armor into the torso; arms originate on the hidden front/side and only the lower outer arms emerge beside the hips. Use crisp dark outlines and high-end hand-painted 2D game-sprite detail on a transparent background. No text, shadow, weapon, extra limbs, duplicated shoulders, twisted feet, long human legs or asymmetric north alignment.
```

Final targeted side-foot correction:

```text
Change only the feet and lower-leg pose of the center WEST character. Both feet must be anatomically consistent bird feet pointing left: all long toes extend left and heels/rear spurs sit to the right. Preserve the SOUTH and NORTH views and every other part of the WEST character exactly.
```

## Blitzkamm south and north turnaround masters

```text
Create one polished horizontal turnaround sheet of exactly three full-body neutral standing views of the same Blitzkamm rooster warrior: SOUTH/front, EAST/right-facing side profile, NORTH/back. Preserve his white face, electric cyan/deep-blue swept comb, sharp eye, yellow beak, blue scarf, blue/silver light armor, blue tail and yellow bird feet. Keep the athletic character compact like the original game sprite. In SOUTH, use a confident shoulder-width stance with both feet straight or slightly outward and absolutely no pigeon toes. In EAST, both feet point right. In NORTH, show heels and toes pointing away. Center the anatomy, attach the tail low, integrate rear shoulders into the torso and hide arm roots toward the front/side. Use crisp dark outlines and high-end hand-painted 2D game-sprite detail on a transparent background. No text, shadow, weapon, extra limbs, inward-facing toes, opposite-facing side feet or long human legs.
```

## Blitzkamm east master

```text
Create exactly one complete full-body EAST/right-facing orthographic side-profile view of Blitzkamm in a shallow readable walking key pose. Show exactly two distinct short bird legs and two separate feet. The beak points right. On both feet, three long front toes extend right and only one small rear spur points left. Place the forward foot farther right and the rear foot farther left while keeping both feet on the same ground line. Preserve the established compact identity, blue/silver costume, low tail, crisp dark outlines and polished hand-painted sprite style. Transparent background; no text, shadow, weapon, extra limbs, merged feet, crossed legs or three-quarter view.
```

Final targeted rear-foot correction:

```text
Change exactly the rear foot on the left side of the image. Keep its ankle fixed and reconstruct the foot so its three long yellow front toes and black claws extend to the right; only the small rear spur extends left. Preserve the already-correct forward foot and every other part of the image exactly.
```
